WHY AN ISLAND CAN VANISH

An island is kept on this device only, in the browser, so nothing about a child ever leaves the iPad. The cost of that is real: Safari clears a website's stored data by itself if the site has not been opened for a while, and a Private tab never keeps anything at all.

The fix is to add it to the Home Screen. Open the island in Safari, tap the share button, choose Add to Home Screen. Opened from the home screen icon it counts as an installed app and Safari stops clearing it. Do that once and this stops happening.
