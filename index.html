Move: WASD or arrows   Throw: space   Pause: P   Restart: R   Sound: M
Touch: press and drag to move
Runaway luggage is loose on the deck and it is mildly panicking.

1. Walk into a bag to grab it (or just shove it along).
2. Haul it to the green Safe Zone at the top of the deck.
3. Keep bags away from the two drains — anything that goes down a drain is fine, but it is also gone.

Quick back-to-back rescues build a combo. Snatching a bag from right beside a drain earns a close call bonus. Every rescue raises the chaos level: the deck tilts harder, wind gusts arrive, and waves start sweeping across. You cannot lose. You can only get funnier.
