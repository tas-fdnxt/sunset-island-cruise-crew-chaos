SUNSET ISLAND

Cruise Crew Chaos
Touch: drag anywhere to steer, tap THROW to toss
Keys: WASD/arrows move   space throw   P pause   R restart   M sound
Runaway luggage is loose on the deck and it is mildly panicking.

1. Walk into a bag to grab it (or just shove it along).
2. Haul it to the green Safe Zone at the top of the deck.
3. Keep bags away from the two drains — anything that goes down a drain is fine, but it is also gone.

Watch for the seagull! It swoops in and drags bags toward the drains. Run up and bonk it to scare it off.

Pick your ship on the title screen: classic sunset, or spooky Storm Night with rain and lightning!

Quick back-to-back rescues build a combo. Snatching a bag from right beside a drain earns a close call bonus. Every rescue raises the chaos level (the strip along the top): the deck tilts harder, wind gusts arrive, and waves start sweeping across. You cannot lose. You can only get funnier.
This prototype does not require an account and does not collect personal information. Nothing is stored or transmitted — closing the tab erases everything. Internal test build, not a public service.
