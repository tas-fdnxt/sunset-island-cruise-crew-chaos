THE BIG ONE

THERE IS A SOCCER PITCH NOW

⚽

Uncle Tabs heard that you love soccer. So your island has a proper pitch, with two goals, and a real ball sitting on the centre spot.

THIS IS YOUR PITCH

a real photo of your island

You get the plan from the GAMES button. Then you build it with your hands for free, or you pay the crew in coins and watch them do it.

It is nine blocks one way and six the other. Count them.

KICK IT IN

a goal going in, for real

Tap the ball and it flies at the far goal. Or get in the car and drive straight at it and it belts off.

Every goal pays you one coin, and every islander on the island stops and cheers.

Only through the middle counts. Past the side is not a goal.

it slides now

There were too many buttons to fit on the screen, and the ones on the end were hiding. That was our mistake.

ALSO NEW

PEOPLE WANT A LIFT

🚕

Stop your car near an islander and they will put their hand up. They tell you where they want to go, like "north, to the lighthouse".

Drive them there and they pay you a coin.

THREE JOBS EVERY DAY

today's three jobs

Press the flag and you get three jobs for today. Here is the good bit: every single island in the whole world gets the exact same three as you.

Nothing ever runs out of time. If you do not finish, they wait.
